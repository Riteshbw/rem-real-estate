## ✨ Architectural & Functional Highlights
- **100% Direct-to-Developer (Zero Brokerage):** Integrated direct telephone dialers and WhatsApp bridges bypassing middleman commissions.
- **Verified Property Intelligence:** 13 verified asset dossiers spanning luxury residential apartments, gated villa plots, and commercial tech hubs with 100% statutory RERA clearances and usable carpet area efficiency scoring.
- **Algorithmic Fractional Co-Ownership:** Micro-equity syndicate simulator partitioning high-value commercial tech parks (Deloitte / AWS IT floors) into standardized 10-share co-investment pools delivering 9.6% net monthly rental yields.
- **Interactive Architectural Lighting Split-Slider:** Real-time Day vs. Twilight architectural lighting comparison viewer.
- **Client-Side Specification Dossier:** Instant browser-based brochure compilation and export (`Blob`).
- **Kinetic UI Animations:** Dual-state rolling navigation links, peer-dimming focus groups, liquid button fills, and inertia zoom photography.
